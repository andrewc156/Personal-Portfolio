Spectral analysis on fractals
About

First-Year Scholar research on eigenvalues of the fractal Laplacian.

How fractal geometry shapes the spectrum of the fractal Laplacian, from the one-dimensional Dirichlet eigenvalue problem to two dimensions.

Position
First-Year Scholar Undergraduate Researcher, Kennesaw State University · Aug 2024 – May 2025
Methods
Formulated the Dirichlet eigenvalue problem for the one-dimensional fractal Laplacian and derived analytical solutions for specific fractal parameters.
Computed eigenvalues and eigenfunctions numerically to show how fractal geometry shapes the spectrum.
Extended the analysis to two dimensions using separation of variables.

Mission

Andrew Chincea studies mathematics (statistics concentration) and data science and analytics at Kennesaw State University. His primary research, on the RiverNets project in the KSU TDA Lab, uses graph-Laplacian regression to predict water quality at river stations with no data. He also works in analytic number theory and has studied eigenvalues of the fractal Laplacian.

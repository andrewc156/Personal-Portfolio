About

The largest kth-power divisor of a random integer, the subject of a submitted manuscript.

The submitted manuscript Largest kth-Power Divisor of a Random Integer establishes an asymptotic formula with an O(log x) error term for every integer k ≥ 2.

Methods
Asymptotic analysis of the largest kth-power divisor of a random integer.
Python experiments on integers up to 10⁸, calculating empirical averages, growth constants and relative errors to validate the theoretical results.
